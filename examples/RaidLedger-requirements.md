# RaidLedger Requirements

This file holds RaidLedger's requirements that were decided after the raid of
2026-10-26. The older loot rules (rolls, tallies, tie-breaks, parking, BoE
rolls, editors) are still only in `RaidLedger/README.md` and its specs; moving
them here is open work (REQUIREMENTS.md FR-14.5).

## Functional Requirements

### FR-RL-001 - Reopening a corpse never adds its loot twice
- **Status:** Implemented (offline)
- **Source:** Raid of 2026-10-26: reopening the boss corpse put its loot into the list several times.
- **Statement:** RaidLedger MUST add each item of a corpse or chest to the loot list once, however often the loot window is opened. An item already listed, rolled or handed out from that corpse MUST NOT be added again. A second copy of the same item on the same corpse MUST still be listed (bundled tokens stay one row).
- **Cause:** Items were recognized by corpse, loot slot and item. After a hand-out the client moves the remaining items up a slot, and a corpse that is not the player's target was named after the latest creature death (a trash kill in between gave it a new name).
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given a corpse with three items, when the master looter hands out the first and opens the corpse again, then the list holds the same three items (one of them awarded) and no new rows. Given an untargeted corpse, when another creature dies and the corpse is opened again, then no item is added. Given a corpse with two copies of one item, when it is opened, then both copies are listed.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/raid_feedback_2026_10_26.spec.lua::FR-RL-001 a corpse reopened after a hand-out lists no item twice`, `::FR-RL-001 an untargeted corpse keeps its name when another creature dies`, `::FR-RL-001 two copies of one item on a corpse are both listed`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/loot_tracking.spec.lua::handouts from the default master-loot menu are recorded, and a reopened corpse lists items once`. Offline only: no plan step reopens a corpse. ASSUMPTION: the 3.3.5a client lists the remaining items from slot 1 on when a corpse is opened again (the emulator lists what the spec passes).

### FR-RL-002 - Group loot is not recorded
- **Status:** Implemented (offline)
- **Source:** User decision, 2026-10-26; replaces the decision of 2026-10-19 that recorded group-loot wins without a spec.
- **Statement:** RaidLedger MUST NOT record items won with group loot (need, greed or disenchant rolls of the client). Only master-loot hand-outs and the ledger's own actions create awards.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given group loot, when the chat says `Bob won: [item]` or `You won: [item]` and then `Bob receives loot: [item]`, then no award is added.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/raid_feedback_2026_10_26.spec.lua::FR-RL-002 an item won with group loot is not recorded`. Offline only.

### FR-RL-003 - No trade tracking
- **Status:** Verified Live
- **Source:** User decision, 2026-10-26 ("macht mehr Ärger als alles andere").
- **Statement:** RaidLedger MUST NOT watch trades. An item the master looter keeps for a roll winner MUST be recorded right away as the winner's award, not as "held until traded". Its chat announcement MUST name both the winner and the current holder as the person from whom the winner picks it up. An item kept without a roll stays an open item (FR-RL-004) until it is rolled; its roll winner then becomes the recipient at once.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given a roll won by Alice, when the master looter gives the item to himself, then the award's recipient is Alice, it counts in Alice's tally, and the raid hears that it goes to Alice and is picked up from the master looter. Given a completed trade, then no award changes.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/fairness.spec.lua::corrections and hand-overs are announced too`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/raid_feedback_2026_10_26.spec.lua::FR-RL-003 an item the master looter keeps for the winner is the winner's award at once`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/open_items.spec.lua::a later roll on an item another raider holds goes to the winner, from the holder`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/bag_roll.spec.lua::the winner is the recipient of the same award at once (no trade tracking)`. The former live run `0424b57ddf78` announced `geht an Flo... - Main-Spec (Test gibt es weiter).`, which does not use the new pickup wording. Live: all 20 plans of the RaidLedger 2.7.0 release passed on 2026-10-31. `RaidLedger-kept-for-winner-acceptance.json` (run `c5dec4914796`) announced `geht an Flo... - Main-Spec (bei Test abholen).`; `RaidLedger-bag-roll-acceptance.json` (run `9a3e010a5be6`) recorded the winner as recipient at once.

### FR-RL-004 - Open items only in BEUTE
- **Status:** Implemented (offline)
- **Source:** User decision, 2026-10-26.
- **Statement:** Open items (parked with a raider or kept without a roll, waiting for their roll) MUST appear only in the BEUTE tab, under OPEN ITEMS. The VERGABEN tab and its count MUST list them only once they are rolled and handed out. A winning roll on an open item MUST record its winner immediately, without a second VERGEBEN click. The website export keeps them as `parked` lines. LEEREN and `/ledger clear` MUST discard every open item completely: it leaves neither a BEUTE row nor an award/export record.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given a parked item, then BEUTE lists it under OPEN ITEMS and VERGABEN does not list it. Given that item's roll is won, then its winner is recorded immediately, VERGABEN lists the award and BEUTE no longer does. Given open items, when LEEREN or `/ledger clear` is used, then no open item remains in BEUTE, VERGABEN or the saved ledger.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/open_items.spec.lua::a later roll on an item another raider holds goes to the winner, from the holder`; `::a later roll on an item the master looter holds goes to the winner at once`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/open_in_loot.spec.lua::a later roll lists the item once, as a roll; the award ends its OPEN row`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/raid_feedback_2026_10_26.spec.lua::FR-RL-004 an open item is listed in BEUTE only, until its roll is won`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/clear_open_items.spec.lua::FR-RL-004 LEEREN removes open items and their saved awards`. Offline only.

### FR-RL-005 - Disenchanter roll
- **Status:** Verified Live
- **Source:** User decision, 2026-10-26.
- **Statement:** An editor MUST be able to start one disenchanter roll per raid (DE ROLL in BEUTE, or `/ledger deroll`). Everyone in the group may roll `/rnd 100`; other ranges are ignored, the highest roll wins (a tie starts the usual tie-break), and the roll counts in no tally. The winner is the raid's disenchanter until NEW RAID, announced to the raid and shared with the other ledgers. An editor can start it only while no other roll runs, since `/rnd` results go to the latest roll. While a disenchanter is set, DE gives the selected item to the disenchanter, whatever name is in the box; without one, DE keeps today's behaviour (the name in the box, else the player).
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given a started disenchanter roll with rolls of 40 and 87 (1-100) and one of 300 (1-300), when it ends, then the 87 roller is the disenchanter and the raid hears it. Given a disenchanter, when the master looter presses DE on an item nobody wanted, then the item goes to the disenchanter as `disenchanted`, counts for nobody, and no roll starts. Given NEW RAID, then no disenchanter is set.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/raid_feedback_2026_10_26.spec.lua::FR-RL-005 the disenchanter roll takes /rnd 100 only and names the raid's disenchanter`, `::FR-RL-005 DE gives an unwanted item to the disenchanter, whatever the box holds`, `::FR-RL-005 NEW RAID forgets the disenchanter`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/loot_flow_deDE.spec.lua::FR-RL-005 the disenchanter roll is German and fits beside the roll buttons` (refused while another roll runs, `forge.layoutIssues` empty, sketch `RaidLedger-de-roll.svg` checked). Live plan `examples/RaidLedger-disenchanter-acceptance.json`. Sharing the disenchanter with other ledgers (`D` message) is offline untested. Live session `c59b3ef83f50` (bridge run `5d8647f6c30f`, 2026-10-26): DE ROLL, Test's 1-1000 roll was ignored, the bot's 1-100 roll made it the disenchanter (`[Loot] Flo... ist der Entzauberer dieses Raids`), and after a roll nobody took, DE gave the item to the bot (`wird entzaubert (Flo...`), state `disenchanted`, confirmed, no Lua errors. An earlier session (`b3bb366082e0`) also gave both to the bot but failed on plan checks that expected the exact name `Flo`; a run before that was disturbed by a manual click.

### FR-RL-006 - BEUTE shows the next step
- **Status:** Implemented (offline)
- **Source:** User feedback, 2026-10-26: a friend could not use the window (an item nobody wanted was stuck, the bottom buttons were unclear).
- **Statement:** BEUTE MUST offer the roll controls (ROLL, NOCHMAL WÜRFELN, free BoE roll) and hand-out controls (a name box with ALLE, a give button that names the recipient, ENTZAUBERN and GILDENBANK) without numbered step headings or text that tells the player what to do next. The status line MUST state only what happened, for example that an item is open, who won, or that nobody wanted it. The relevant action remains highlighted: ROLL before a roll, the give button after a win, ENTZAUBERN when nobody wanted the item. The give button MUST name the recipient (`VERGEBEN AN BOB`); with an empty box it is disabled and reads `VERGEBEN AN ...`. FREE BOE ROLL is shown only for bind-on-equip items.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given an item nobody rolled on, then the state line says only that nobody wanted it, without `Weiter:` or another instruction, while ENTZAUBERN is highlighted and enabled. Given a won roll, then the state line names the winner without an instruction and the give button reads `VERGEBEN AN <winner>` and is highlighted. Given an open item, then its state line names its holder without an instruction. BEUTE shows neither `1 WÜRFELN` nor `2 VERGEBEN`. Given a German client with full data, then `forge.layoutIssues` finds nothing.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/loot_steps_deDE.spec.lua::FR-RL-006 BEUTE states the loot result without numbered steps or instructions` checks the German unrolled, won, open and unwanted states, absence of the two headings, highlights and empty German layout issues; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/bag_roll.spec.lua::the winner is the recipient of the same award at once (no trade tracking)`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/boe_free_roll.spec.lua::only /rnd 100 counts and the highest roll wins, whatever the tallies`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/ui_countdown_park.spec.lua::the roll countdown in the window ticks down without new rolls`. Offline only. Live session `c18da0d451ba` (bridge run `06c45772a69a`, 2026-10-26, plan `RaidLedger-open-in-loot`) predates the simplified language; it showed the previous labelled layout. The highlight after a win or with nobody is offline only.

### FR-RL-007 - Every item in BEUTE can be handed out
- **Status:** Verified Live
- **Source:** User feedback, 2026-10-26 (screenshot: an open item with "NIEMAND" offered only ROLL AGAIN).
- **Statement:** Every item in BEUTE - corpse loot, an open item, and an open item whose later roll ended - MUST offer the same hand-outs: give to a name, ENTZAUBERN, GILDENBANK and PARKEN. ### FR-RL-009 - VERGABEN only corrects
- **Status:** Implemented (offline)
- **Source:** User feedback, 2026-10-26 ("bei Vergabe gibt's dann plötzlich wieder Parken, DE und Bank").
- **Statement:** VERGABEN MUST NOT offer PARK, DE or BANK. A selected award offers: counts as main spec / off spec / nothing, change recipient, ZURÜCK IN BEUTE (the award becomes an open item with its current holder, to be rolled or handed out again in BEUTE), and remove or restore. The internal `manual` state MUST NOT be shown in the award list, selected-award line or tooltip. The recipient and correction actions use one two-column grid: EMPFÄNGER ÄNDERN is exactly above ENTFERNEN, while the name box is above ZURÜCK IN BEUTE. Both columns grow equally when the window is made wider, with a 6 px gap and 12 px margins.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given a recorded award, when ZURÜCK IN BEUTE is pressed, then it leaves VERGABEN and BEUTE lists it under OPEN ITEMS with its holder. VERGABEN has no PARK, DE or BANK button and does not display MANUELL. Given the recipient and action rows at the default size and after the window grows, then EMPFÄNGER ÄNDERN and ENTFERNEN have identical left and right edges, their left-column controls have identical visual edges, both columns have equal widths, the gap is 6 px, and the right margin is 12 px.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/loot_steps_deDE.spec.lua::FR-RL-009 VERGABEN only corrects: no park, DE or bank button` checks the action grid at the default size and after a 200 px resize: matching recipient/back-to-loot and change/remove column edges, equal lower widths, 6 px gaps and a 12 px right margin; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/corrections_export.spec.lua::clicking an award in the list lets you change its recipient`; `examples/RaidLedger/tests/tabs.spec.lua::VERGABEN: COUNTS AS moves an award between the tallies`. Offline only. Previous live evidence covers the action behaviour only.

### FR-RL-010 - Rolls are announced as raid warnings
- **Status:** Implemented (offline)
- **Source:** User decision, 2026-10-26 ("die Rolls immer über Raidwarnung, damit die Leute es besser mitbekommen").
- **Statement:** In a raid where the player is leader or assistant, the start of every roll (item roll, free BoE roll, later roll, disenchanter roll) and every tie-break MUST be sent as a raid warning (`RAID_WARNING`). Reminders, winners and awards stay in raid chat. Without the right to send raid warnings, or in a party, the announcement goes to raid or party chat as before.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given a raid assistant, when a roll starts, then its announcement is sent as `RAID_WARNING`. Given a plain raid member who may start rolls (editor list), then it is sent to `RAID`. Given a party, then to `PARTY`.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/loot_steps_deDE.spec.lua::FR-RL-010 an assistant announces roll starts and tie-breaks as raid warnings`, `::FR-RL-010 a raid member without rank announces in raid chat`. Offline only: the live fixture forms a party, not a raid.

### FR-RL-014 - A compact assignment window opens with master loot
- **Status:** Verified Live
- **Source:** User decision, 2026-10-27: assigning a corpse item needs its own small window, not the full RaidLedger window or Blizzard's right-click menu.
- **Statement:** When this player is the master looter and opens a corpse with assignable items, RaidLedger MUST show a compact, independent assignment window. It MUST leave the main RaidLedger window closed when it was closed, use the same scale as the RaidLedger window, and be movable by dragging its title area. The window lists the current corpse items, visibly selects a clicked row, and lets the master looter use WÜRFELN, direct recipient selection (**ZUWEISEN**) or **SPÄTER WÜRFELN** on that selected item. Its three actions sit immediately below the visible item rows, not above unused panel space. It shows all ten corpse items that RaidLedger tracks at once. Recipient selection groups the raid by group. A later holder receives the corpse item through `GiveMasterLoot` and is recorded as `parked`; direct Blizzard hand-outs remain recorded by the existing `GiveMasterLoot` hook. The popup MUST close when the loot window closes or has no assignable item left. The Raidliste used for recipient selection MUST stay compact and fit its popup without overlaps.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given a closed RaidLedger main window and an opened master-loot corpse, then the compact assignment window is visible and the main window stays hidden. Given a RaidLedger scale of 80% or 150%, when the master-loot popup opens, then its frame scale is the same. Given two corpse items, when the second row is clicked, then it is visibly selected and ZUWEISEN or SPÄTER WÜRFELN acts on it. Given one corpse item, then the popup is only high enough for its title, item and actions. Given an active corpse item, then the popup lists it and exposes only the three assignment actions. Given ten corpse items, then all ten are visible and the popup has no overlaps or overflow. Given a title-area drag, then the popup moves by that amount. Given a raid member selected for SPÄTER WÜRFELN, then that member receives the item by master loot and the ledger records an open `parked` award. Given a full 25-player raid and long German names, then `forge.layoutIssues("RaidLedgerMasterLoot")` reports nothing.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/master_loot_popup.spec.lua::FR-RL-014 master loot opens a compact assignment popup and records its actions`; `::FR-RL-014 a corpse with many items fills the popup without empty space`; `::FR-RL-014 the compact recipient chooser fits a full raid with long German names`; `::FR-RL-014 the master-loot popup follows the ledger scale`; `::FR-RL-014 the popup removes blank rows when the client loot list shrinks`; `::FR-RL-014 clicking another corpse row selects and assigns that item`. Session `10e2868130c6` (bridge run `98752b4d4e1f`) remains evidence for the former five-action, non-movable popup. The former `GroupLootDropDown` route was rejected after sessions `2b63eca8f3c4`, `e74aa8471b14` and `4d2f503ccd3d` did not expose a dropdown line; it is removed rather than retained as a second interface. ### FR-RL-015 - Master-loot records confirmed hand-outs only
- **Status:** Implemented (offline)
- **Source:** User feedback, 2026-10-27: attempting to give a quest-restricted Soul Shard created two ledger entries even though the client did not hand it out.

### FR-RL-016 - Open token bundles roll together
- **Status:** Implemented (offline)
- **Source:** User feedback, 2026-10-27.
- **Statement:** Open copies of the same token MUST appear as one `Nx <item>` row under OPEN ITEMS, even when they were handed to holders separately. Starting its roll MUST announce the `Nx <item>` bundle and decide all copies together: the highest N valid rollers each receive one copy. The ledger records those winners at once; each record keeps its original holder so the winner knows who has the physical token. A bundle nobody rolled on MUST go back to OPEN ITEMS as one row with no award changed, ready for another roll.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given five open copies of one token, then BEUTE shows exactly one `5x <item>` row. Given six valid rollers, when its roll starts, then the raid announcement names the `5x <item>` bundle. When its roll ends, then the best five each receive one token and the sixth stays without one. Given no rolls on an open bundle, when its roll ends, then the raid hears that nobody wanted it, the bundle's hand-out buttons stay disabled, its copies stay `parked` with their holders, and ROLL can start again.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/token_bundles.spec.lua::one roll: the best three win a token each, announced together`; `::FR-RL-016 open copies of one token roll together for the best five`, `::FR-RL-016 an open token bundle nobody rolled on goes back to OPEN ITEMS`. Offline only.

### FR-RL-019 - A loot method button appears when the loot method does not fit the boss fight
- **Status:** Implemented (offline; click and text verified live, appearing when needed offline only)
- **Source:** User decisions, 2026-11-01: a reminder with one click instead of an automatic switch; then shown only when needed; then based only on boss-fight detection, to try out.
- **Statement:** Inside an instance, the leader of a party or raid MUST get a small, movable on-screen button, independent of the main window, when the loot method does not fit: in a boss fight without master loot, and outside a boss fight with master loot. A boss fight starts when a `boss1`-`boss4` unit exists, or when the player is in combat and the player's target or a group member's target has classification `worldboss`. It lasts until the player leaves combat and no boss unit exists. What the player targets otherwise does not matter. The button MUST hide once the method fits, and for anyone else. Its text names the current method and the change, starts with `!` and it is red (`! BEUTE: GRUPPE > PLÜNDERMEISTER`). The method comes from the client (`GetLootMethod`): the text and the hiding change only when the client reports it (`PARTY_LOOT_METHOD_CHANGED`), never on the click alone. A click MUST switch master loot to group loot, and any other method to master loot with the player as master looter (by name, not by unit). The loot threshold is not changed.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given a raid led by Tester on group loot inside an instance, when a boss fight starts, then the button shows `! BEUTE: GRUPPE > PLÜNDERMEISTER`; a click calls `SetLootMethod("master", "Tester")`, and it stays until the client reports the change, then hides. Given master loot, when the fight ends, then it shows `! BEUTE: PLÜNDERMEISTER > GRUPPE` and a click calls `SetLootMethod("group")`. Given a fight with a boss that a raid member targets, when the player's target changes to an add, then it is still a boss fight. Given master loot in a fight with trash only, then the button shows. Given group loot outside a boss fight, master loot in a boss fight, master loot outside an instance, or a player who leads no group, then it is hidden. Given a German client and a long name, then `forge.layoutIssues` finds nothing. Given a drag, then the button moves.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/loot_method_button.spec.lua`. Live plan `examples/RaidLedger-loot-method-acceptance.json`, bridge run `3ea78443b3b0` (2026-11-01, party led by Test, before the button hid itself): two clicks changed the method to group loot and back, the client confirmed both (`Plündern in "Plündern als Gruppe" geändert.`, `Plündern in "Plündermeister" geändert.`, `Test ist jetzt der Plündermeister.`), the button text followed in the screenshots, no Lua errors. This also shows that `SetLootMethod("master", <name>)` works live. The user tried the boss-fight version in the test client on 2026-11-01 and reported that it works (manual test, no run ID or recorded evidence). No automated live run covers appearing and hiding by boss fight (no fixture reaches an instance), a raid (the fixture forms a party), or dragging. ASSUMPTION: every raid boss the guild fights is classified `worldboss` or has a `boss1` unit. Known effect, accepted to try it: right after a kill the fight is over, so with master loot still on the button asks for group loot before the corpse is looted.

- **Status:** Implemented (offline)

- **Statement:** RAID MUST have a switch for whisper invites, off by default and saved, and a box for the word, default `inv`, at most 20 letters and saved; an empty box means `inv`. While the switch is on and the player may invite, a whisper of exactly that word (any case, spaces around it ignored) MUST invite its sender when the sender (taken from the event, SC-019) is on the invite list, not in the group and not already invited. Bench players count only with BENCH: YES. Any other whisper, and any sender not on the list, MUST be ignored without an answer. The invite goes through the same queue as INVITE ALL, so a party still turns into a raid.

Given the word `Komm`, when Healer whispers `komm`, then Healer is invited, and `inv` no longer invites. Given an emptied box, then `inv` works again. Given a stranger, a bench player without BENCH: YES, a whisper of `inv please`, the switch off, or a raid member without rights, then nobody is invited and no whisper is sent. Given a German client with a full list, then `forge.layoutIssues` finds nothing.

### FR-RL-022 - Sync says why another raid is not merged and offers to switch
- **Status:** Implemented (offline)
- **Source:** User report 2026-11-06 (a character that was not in Monday's raid ran `/ledger sync`; nothing arrived and nothing said why) and user decision 2026-11-06: say why every time, and offer to switch.
- **Statement:** When a sync the player asked for (`/ledger sync` or SYNC AWARDS) brings awards of another raid while this ledger has awards of its own, RaidLedger MUST say so in chat every time, naming the sender, and MUST ask once per sync and raid whether to replace this ledger with that raid. YES MUST keep this ledger's raid under HISTORY (FR-RL-023), make that raid the current one and ask the group again, so its awards arrive. NO MUST change nothing. Since 2.10.0 the other raid is also kept under HISTORY, and outside a sync of the player's own the question comes once per raid (FR-RL-026: the player is always told and decides). An empty ledger keeps following the sender's raid without a question (unchanged).
- **Rationale:** Players switch characters between raid days, and a ledger that silently keeps its own raid looks broken.
- **Security:** Only group members' ledgers are asked; with an editor list, only editors' ledgers count (unchanged). The question names the sender from the event (SC-019). Replacing the ledger needs the player's click.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given a ledger with its own awards, when the player syncs and another raid's ledger answers, then chat names that ledger and a question appears; a second sync says it again. Given YES, then this ledger's raid is kept under HISTORY, that raid is current, and the awards sent next are stored. Given NO, then the current raid stays. Given awards of another raid without a sync of the player's own, then the question appears once.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/shared_ledger.spec.lua::FR-RL-022 a sync explains another raid every time and offers to switch`. Offline only.

### FR-RL-023 - Finished raids are kept, not deleted
- **Status:** Implemented (offline; NEW RAID keeping the raid verified live)
- **Source:** User question 2026-11-06 ("store multiple raids so in the end we might even be able to have a history of them in the addon").
- **Statement:** NEW RAID MUST close the current raid and keep it as a saved raid instead of deleting it: its awards with their roll logs, its tally corrections, its raid key (`session`), start time, website raid ID and disenchanter. The new raid starts empty, as today. How many saved raids RaidLedger keeps MUST be a setting the player chooses, with no fixed upper limit (decided 2026-11-06, OQ-RL-1). Beyond that number it drops the oldest. Each raid keeps the current award cap of 250. A ledger saved before this feature MUST keep its awards as the current raid.
- **Rationale:** Raids span several days, and raid leads want to look back at earlier raids without an export.
- **Security:** Saved raids are local data. Their size stays bounded by the raid and award caps (SC-019).
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given a current raid with awards, when the player confirms NEW RAID, then the ledger is empty and the old raid is listed as a saved raid with all its awards. Given a ledger saved by RaidLedger 2.9.0, when the new version loads it, then its awards are the current raid. Given more saved raids than the limit, then the oldest one is dropped.
- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/raid_history.spec.lua::FR-RL-023 NEW RAID keeps the finished raid with all its awards`, `::FR-RL-023 the player chooses how many raids to keep`; `tests/migration.spec.lua::old awards get IDs oldest first and appear in the export` (a 2.9.0 ledger). The default of 20 kept raids is an `ASSUMPTION` until NFR-RL-001's live check. Live: plan `RaidLedger-history-acceptance.json`, session `39e6f3124f56` (bridge run `0f451cf0f6ce`, screenshots `step-history_tab`, `step-history_awards`) and again in the 2.10.0 release (run `8679e12f91be`), 2026-11-07.

### FR-RL-026 - A sync from another raid is kept as a saved raid

### FR-RL-030 - Share a finished raid with the group

- **Evidence:** `examples/RaidLedger/tests/share_raid.spec.lua::FR-RL-030 SHARE without a group sends nothing and says so`, `::FR-RL-030 SHARE sends the saved raid's awards and logs, marked as finished`, `::FR-RL-030 a shared raid is kept under VERLAUF in date order, told once, never offered`, `::FR-RL-030 a shared raid that is our current raid joins it`, `::FR-RL-030 one sender adds at most 5 shared raids per login`; sketch `history-share`.
